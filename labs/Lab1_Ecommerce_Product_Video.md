# Lab 1: Unbox the Wireless Earbuds

**Topic:** AI E-commerce Product Videos  
**Tool:** OmniMedia → Avatar → Product Ad Video  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Video format:** 9:16 (vertical)

## Goal

Create a short, hands-on video that reveals wireless earbuds and their charging case. Use the actual product image and highlight **three confirmed product features**.

## Before You Start

You will need:

- An OmniMedia account with **Standard or higher** access to Avatar Studio and enough credits to generate a video.
- The sample assets pre-loaded in `sample-assets/IMGs/` (the required product image, plus an optional avatar presenter image), or your own assets.
- Three confirmed features. The ready-to-use prompt uses visible design details. For other claims, check the packaging, manual, or official product listing.

## Sample Assets

The sample assets for this lab are pre-loaded in the [`sample-assets/IMGs/`](../sample-assets/IMGs) folder:

| **Product Image** | **Required** | [wireless-earbuds-reference.png](../sample-assets/IMGs/wireless-earbuds-reference.png) | Clear product reference showing two stem-style earbuds, silicone ear tips, and flip-top charging case |

## Step 1: Open Product Ad Video

1. Sign in to **OmniMedia**.
2. Open **Avatar** in the top navigation.
3. Under **Create**, select **Product Ad Video**.

## Step 2: Add Your Product Image (and Optional Avatar)

1. Click **Upload** in the **Product** box.
2. Select **Uploads → Browse Files** and choose `sample-assets/IMGs/wireless-earbuds-reference.png`, or your own product image.
3. Check the preview, then close the popup.
4. Confirm that the image appears in the **Product** box.
5. *(Optional)* If you want an on-screen human presenter in the ad, click **Upload** in the **Avatar** box and choose `sample-assets/IMGs/sample-avatar-reference.png`. Otherwise, leave the **Avatar** box empty to produce a hands-only demonstration.

If you want to show a retail-box unboxing, include that packaging in the reference image. Otherwise, use a charging-case reveal.

## Step 3: Enter the Ad Concept

Choose one of the prompts below and paste it into **“Describe your product ad concept...”**.

### Template Prompt

Use this for your own product. Replace every item in square brackets with accurate details.

```text
Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 product ad for [PRODUCT NAME].
Use the uploaded image to keep the earbuds and charging case consistent
in shape, color, and visible branding.

Show a realistic hands-only unboxing on a clean tabletop with soft natural
light. If the reference includes the retail box, show hands opening it.
Otherwise, start with the charging case on the table.

Open the charging case, reveal both earbuds, and gently lift one out.
Use close-up shots to highlight these three confirmed features, one at
a time, with short captions:
1. [FEATURE 1]
2. [FEATURE 2]
3. [FEATURE 3]

Finish with both earbuds beside the open case and the words
"Explore the earbuds."

Keep the product clearly visible in the vertical frame. Use smooth hand
movements. Include only the supplied product details and feature claims.
```

The prompt requests the actions and captions. Check the generated video to see whether they appear correctly.

### Ready-to-Use Prompt

Use this with the sample image described above. It is ready to copy and paste, with no placeholders to replace. Confirm that all three design features are visible in your image first.

```text
Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 product ad for the wireless earbuds in the
uploaded reference image. Match their shape, color, and visible branding.

Show a hands-only charging-case reveal on a clean tabletop with soft natural
light. Hands pick up the case, open the lid to reveal both earbuds, and
gently lift one earbud out.

Use three close-up shots with these short captions:
1. "Stem-style design" — show the shape of an earbud.
2. "Silicone ear tips" — show a close-up of the ear tips.
3. "Flip-open charging case" — show both earbuds inside the open case.

Finish with both earbuds beside the open charging case and the words
"Explore the earbuds."

Use smooth, natural hand movements. Keep the product and captions clearly
visible. Show only the design features in the reference image; do not add
technical specifications or extra accessories.
```

## Step 4: Choose the Settings

| Setting | Select |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aspect Ratio** | **9:16** |
| **Quality** | **Standard** |
| **Duration** | **15s** |

Check the credit estimate beside **Generate** before continuing. Use **Standard** quality for this lab because it supports 15 seconds.

## Step 5: Generate the Video

1. Check the product image, completed prompt, and settings.
2. Click **Generate** once.
3. Wait for generation to finish in **History**.
4. Open **History → Creations** and play the completed video.

## Step 6: Check and Download

Before downloading, check that:

- [ ] The earbuds and case match the uploaded image.
- [ ] The hands clearly reveal the earbuds and charging case.
- [ ] All three confirmed features are communicated correctly, with readable captions.
- [ ] The video is vertical **9:16**, and the product stays visible.
- [ ] The hand movements and product shape look natural throughout.

If something is wrong, revise that part of the prompt and generate again. Reattach the product image if the form has cleared. Check the credit estimate before each new generation.

When satisfied, use **Download** on the completed creation. Open the downloaded file to confirm it plays.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Generate** is disabled | Add a product image and enter a prompt. Wait if a generation is already running. |
| A plan or credit message appears | Check your Avatar Studio access and available credits with the instructor. |
| **15s** is unavailable | Change **Quality** to **Standard**. |
| The product looks different | Use a clearer reference and ask for fewer shots and simpler movements. |
| Captions are missing or incorrect | Shorten the feature text, request one caption at a time, and review the next result. |
| The completed video is missing | Check **History → Creations**. Refresh after the task has finished if needed. |

## Expected Result

A downloaded **9:16 wireless-earbuds product ad** showing the earbuds, charging case, and three accurate features. Share the video with your instructor and explain how you confirmed the features using the product image or product information.
