# Lab 4: One Problem, One Faster Solution

**Topic:** AI Performance Marketing Videos  
**Tool:** OmniMedia → Avatar → UGC Videos  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Video format:** 9:16 (vertical)

## Goal

Create a high-converting 15-second direct-response performance ad using the proven **Problem–Solution–CTA** structure: open with a relatable work frustration, transition to a product demonstration featuring approved software screenshots, and finish with a compelling promotional offer card and clear call-to-action.

## Before You Start

You will need:

- An OmniMedia account with access to **Avatar Studio** and sufficient credits for UGC video generation.
- The sample assets pre-loaded in `sample-assets/IMGs/` (service product screenshot and customer avatar image), or your own brand assets.
- Approved product details and a clear call-to-action offer. (The ready-to-use prompt below uses a productivity SaaS workflow with a 50% discount code).

## Sample Assets

The sample assets for this lab are pre-loaded in the [`sample-assets/IMGs/`](../sample-assets/IMGs) folder:

| Asset Type | Requirement | File Link | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product / UI Screenshot** | **Required** | [service-app-screenshot.png](../sample-assets/IMGs/service-app-screenshot.png) | High-resolution vertical UI screenshot demonstrating 1-click automation and a 50% off offer card |
| **Creator / Customer Avatar** | **Required** | [creator-avatar-reference.png](../sample-assets/IMGs/creator-avatar-reference-2.png) | Relatable creator avatar representing the customer sharing the solution |

## Step 1: Open UGC Videos

1. Sign in to **OmniMedia**.
2. Open **Avatar** in the top navigation bar.
3. Under the **Create** column in the dropdown menu, select **UGC Videos**.

## Step 2: Add Your Assets (Product Screenshot & Avatar)

1. Click **Upload** in the **Product** box.
2. Select **Uploads → Browse Files** and select `sample-assets/IMGs/service-app-screenshot.png`.
3. Confirm that the service screenshot appears in the Product slot.
4. Click **Upload** in the **Avatar** box.
5. Select `sample-assets/IMGs/creator-avatar-reference.png` and confirm it appears in the Avatar slot.

## Step 3: Enter the Ad Concept

Choose one of the prompts below and paste it into **“Describe your UGC video concept...”**.

### Template Prompt

Use this template for your own SaaS, mobile app, or online service:

```text
Create a 15-second performance ad for [SERVICE] with a problem–solution–CTA structure. First show a relatable frustration, then a clear product demonstration using approved screenshots, and finally a compelling offer card. Use brisk pacing, strong first-two-second hook, and clean subtitles.

Timeline:
- Problem Hook (0–4s): Creator avatar looks stressed at desk: "Still doing [MANUAL TASK] by hand in 2026? Stop."
- Solution (4–11s): Quick split-screen cut to the uploaded screenshot showing [KEY FEATURE]. Creator gestures enthusiastically: "[SERVICE] automates everything in one single click."
- CTA & Offer (11–15s): Zoom in on the offer card banner. Bold on-screen caption: "Try it free + 50% off with code [PROMO CODE]". Creator points to the link button: "Tap below to get started!"

Style: Authentic creator UGC, punchy direct-response pacing, high-contrast subtitles, energetic delivery.
```

### Ready-to-Use Prompt

Use this prompt directly with the provided sample assets:

```text
Create a 15-second performance ad for SwiftFlow AI with a problem–solution–CTA structure. First show a relatable frustration, then a clear product demonstration using approved screenshots, and finally a compelling offer card. Use brisk pacing, strong first-two-second hook, and clean subtitles.

Scene breakdown:
1. Hook (0–4s): Creator avatar looking overwhelmed at their laptop screen with bold caption: "Stop wasting 5 hours every week on manual data entry!"
2. Solution (4–11s): Rapid transition revealing the uploaded SwiftFlow dashboard screenshot. Creator gestures toward the screen highlighting 1-click automation with subtitles: "SwiftFlow syncs your workflows and tasks automatically."
3. CTA & Offer (11–15s): Cut to the high-contrast offer card banner. Bold animated caption: "Limited Time: GET 50% OFF · USE CODE: FASTER50". Creator gives a thumbs-up and points to the link below: "Claim your discount today!"

Pacing: Snappy cuts, direct camera address, crisp legible typography, vertical 9:16 performance ad framing.
```

## Step 4: Choose the Settings

Configure the generation parameters:

| Setting | Select | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspect Ratio** | **9:16** | Vertical orientation tailored for direct-response paid social ads |
| **Quality** | **Standard** | Balanced quality supporting 15-second ad runtimes |
| **Duration** | **15s** | High-retention format designed for paid feed placement |

Review the estimated credits beside **Generate** before submitting.

## Step 5: Generate the Video

1. Verify that both the **Product** screenshot and **Avatar** image are selected.
2. Confirm the prompt and settings (**9:16**, **Standard**, **15s**).
3. Click **Generate** once.
4. Track generation progress in the notification tray or **History**.
5. Once completed, navigate to **History → Creations** to view the rendered ad.

## Step 6: Review and Download

Audit your completed performance ad against this conversion checklist:

- [ ] **First 2 Seconds Hook:** Does the video immediately grab attention with a relatable user pain point?
- [ ] **Product Visibility:** Is the SwiftFlow dashboard screenshot clearly visible during the solution segment?
- [ ] **Subtitles & Text:** Are captions bold, legible, and synchronized with the speech?
- [ ] **Offer Card Clarity:** Is the closing discount offer (`FASTER50`) prominently displayed before the video loops?
- [ ] **Pacing:** Does the entire 15-second sequence move briskly without dead space?

When satisfied, click **Download** to save the video file (MP4).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Generate button remains disabled** | Both the **Product** screenshot and **Avatar** image must be uploaded, and the prompt text cannot be empty. |
| **15s duration option is greyed out** | Ensure **Quality** is set to **Standard** (Premium quality may restrict maximum clip duration). |
| **Text captions are cut off on screen edges** | Add the phrase *"Keep captions centered within safe vertical margins"* to your prompt. |
| **Avatar delivery feels too stiff** | Add adjectives like *"enthusiastic"*, *"authentic UGC style"*, and *"energetic expressions"* into the concept prompt. |

## Expected Result

A downloaded **15-second 9:16 vertical performance marketing ad** featuring a relatable problem hook, clear demonstration of the SwiftFlow dashboard screenshot, bold on-screen subtitles, and a closing promo code offer card.
