# OmniMedia AI Creative Studio Workshop

> **Workshop:** Attend the live session or follow along at your own pace. All lab files and sample assets are included in this repository.

Welcome to the **OmniMedia** hands-on workshop! This repository guides you through creating professional AI-generated media using **OmniMedia** — an all-in-one AI creative studio featuring comprehensive toolsets for Image, Video, Avatar, Story, 3D, Voice, and Music generation and editing.

In this workshop, you will learn hands-on workflows across OmniMedia's studios:
- **Avatar Studio (Product Ad Video)**: Generating AI e-commerce product videos from reference images.
- **Video Studio (Ingredients to Video - 9:16)**: Composing high-impact social media reels from brand assets.
- **Video Studio (Ingredients to Video - 16:9)**: Producing widescreen corporate training videos with presenter and educational mockups.
- **Avatar Studio (UGC Videos - Performance Marketing)**: Creating direct-response performance marketing ads with the Problem–Solution–CTA framework.
- **Avatar Studio (Script Avatar - 16:9)**: Animating branded virtual customer support hosts and digital humans with synchronized speech.
- **Avatar Studio (UGC Videos - Dual Conditioning)**: Creating authentic creator-led UGC and influencer product showcase videos with dual reference conditioning.
- **Avatar Studio (Video Translation)**: Localizing and dubbing existing video tutorials into multiple languages with voice cloning and lip-syncing.
- **Avatar Studio (Script Avatar - 9:16)**: Creating high-touch 1-to-1 personalized customer welcome and marketing videos.
- **Avatar Studio (AI Influencer)**: Developing consistent virtual brand ambassadors and generating styled fashion and lifestyle video scenes.
- **Avatar Studio (Multi Character Videos)**: Orchestrating dynamic multi-character dialogues and customer interview videos with automated speaker switching.

## Workshop Syllabus & Modules

Work through the labs in order. Each module focuses on a real-world commercial creative workflow:

| Module | Topic | Estimated Time | Lab File | What You Will Do |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Lab 1** | E-commerce Product Video (Unbox the Wireless Earbuds) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 1: E-commerce Product Video](./labs/Lab1_Ecommerce_Product_Video.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → Product Ad Video**, upload a product reference image (plus optional avatar presenter), craft an ad concept prompt, configure parameters (9:16 vertical, Standard quality, 15s), and generate a realistic hands-on product unboxing video. |
| **Lab 2** | AI Social Media Videos & Reels (3 Reasons to Try This Coffee) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 2: Social Media Reel](./labs/Lab2_Social_Media_Reel.md) | Navigate to **Video → Ingredients to Video**, attach brand assets (OmniRoast logo and optional pour reference), craft a multi-scene reel prompt with 3 verified selling points, configure vertical 9:16 settings with the **Kling V3 Omni** model, and generate a dynamic, caption-driven social reel. |
| **Lab 3** | AI Corporate Training Videos (Workplace Cybersecurity: Three Habits) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 3: Corporate Training Video](./labs/Lab3_Corporate_Training_Video.md) | Navigate to **Video → Ingredients to Video**, attach the instructor reference and phishing email mockup, configure widescreen 16:9 settings with the **Veo 3.1** model, enter a 60-second structured cybersecurity script (teaching 3 habits with realistic threat demonstration), and generate an enterprise training video. |
| **Lab 4** | AI Performance Marketing Videos (One Problem, One Faster Solution) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 4: Performance Marketing Ad](./labs/Lab4_Performance_Marketing_Ad.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → UGC Videos**, upload a software UI screenshot and a creator avatar, enter a 15-second direct-response prompt structured around Problem–Solution–CTA, set 9:16 vertical parameters, and generate an authentic, high-converting social performance ad. |
| **Lab 5** | AI Avatar & Digital Human Videos (Meet Your Virtual Support Host) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 5: Virtual Support Host](./labs/Lab5_Virtual_Support_Host.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → Script Avatar**, select a digital support host avatar, attach the synchronized voiceover track, enter the branded support channels script, set widescreen 16:9 parameters, and generate a realistic talking digital human host. |
| **Lab 6** | AI UGC & Influencer-Style Videos (Daily Productivity Planner Showcase) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 6: AI UGC Influencer Video](./labs/Lab6_AI_UGC_Influencer_Video.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → UGC Videos**, upload physical product and creator avatar images, craft an action-oriented UGC concept with quoted dialogue and ending tagline, configure 9:16 vertical Premium (8s) settings, and generate a photorealistic creator product review. |
| **Lab 7** | AI Video Localization & Dubbing (One Tutorial, Two Languages) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 7: AI Video Localization Dubbing](./labs/Lab7_AI_Video_Localization_Dubbing.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → Video Translation**, upload a source tutorial video, choose target language (e.g. Spanish) and Precision processing mode, and automatically dub and lip-sync the video with speaker voice likeness without writing prompts. |
| **Lab 8** | AI Personalized Marketing Videos (A Welcome Just for You) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 8: AI Personalized Marketing Videos](./labs/Lab8_AI_Personalized_Marketing_Videos.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → Script Avatar**, upload a vertical presenter avatar, input a ~45–50 word personalized customer welcome script (~20 seconds), and generate a 1-to-1 customer onboarding video with natural lip synchronization. |
| **Lab 9** | AI Virtual Influencer Videos (Meet Nova, Your Virtual Style Guide) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 9: AI Virtual Influencer Video](./labs/Lab9_AI_Virtual_Influencer_Video.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → AI Influencer**, upload a persona reference portrait for Nova, generate face-consistent fashion scenes, and animate the chosen look into a 9:16 vertical video with Dolly-In camera motion. |
| **Lab 10** | Multi-Character Dialogue Videos (A Customer Story: Q&A Interview) | ~5–10 min | [Lab 10: AI Multi-Character Video](./labs/Lab10_AI_Multi_Character_Video.md) | Navigate to **Avatar → Multi Character Videos**, configure two distinct speakers (host and client) with custom portraits and voices, enter a structured dialogue script with speaker tags (`Maya:` and `David:`), and generate an automated multi-speaker interview video. |
